## Corrections and limitations

Selected RED/GREEN cycles are recorded in [projection evidence](projection-tdd-evidence.md) and [scene evidence](scene-evidence.md). Main review required accepting monotone sequence gaps, snapping interrupted cosmetic movement, readable labels, explicit order units, rejection of malformed traces before state changes, and correction of camera reentrancy. The camera regression first showed identical pixels and `Maximum call stack size exceeded`: `render` called `controls.update`, whose change handler called `render` again. The corrected path renders without reentrant updates; damping is disabled. The final full suite passed after these corrections.

Earlier `main-camera-zoom/closer/closest.png`, `main-first-scene.png` and `main-mobile.png` capture superseded or pre-fix states and are not final acceptance images. Missing video support did not replace any required test runner.

No acceptance check remains failed or skipped.
